Orbit Flip Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy covers Orbit Flip for Android (package name com.orbitflip.app), made by Aurelian Interactive ("we", "us"). It explains what information the game handles and what happens to it.

The short version

The current version of Orbit Flip does not collect, send or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not ask for permission to use the internet.

What the game stores on your phone

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file in its own private storage on your device. It contains:

  • your best score and the number of runs you have played
  • the color palette you selected
  • your sound and music settings

This file never leaves your phone. We cannot see it, and it is not included in Android backups. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, used for a short buzz when you crash. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or any other personal data.

Google Play

If you install Orbit Flip from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under its own privacy policy. Google gives us summary statistics such as install counts, and crash and performance reports from users who have chosen to share them with app developers. These reports do not tell us who you are.

Children

Orbit Flip is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit. See GitHub's privacy statement. The site itself uses no cookies, trackers or analytics.

Changes to this policy

We plan to add ads (Google AdMob) and an optional one-time purchase to remove them in a future version. Those features involve data handled by Google. We will update this policy before that version is released, describe exactly what changes, and update the effective date above.
